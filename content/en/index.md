Hello and welcome to my site.

I'm Georges Cubas, who fell into programming after my father bought an [Amstrad CPC 464](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Amstrad_CPC_464) in the mid-1980s.

You'll mostly find articles on that subject here, though hopefully not only that.

This must be the fourth version of this site. It started around 2006 as a blog on the [Opera](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Opera_\(web_browser\)) platform, which I liked a lot. Unfortunately I lost the content. I then moved to [Tumblr](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tumblr) (it was the trend at the time), then installed [WordPress](https://wordpress.org/), and finally this version, a [static site](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Static_web_page) generated with [Quartz](https://quartz.jzhao.xyz/).

Enjoy your visit.
